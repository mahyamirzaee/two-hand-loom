The Two-Hand Loom

A loom that only weaves when two people touch it at the same time.

  1. Hold the screen between you.
  2. Each of you, rest a finger on one edge.
  3. Stay together. The thread moves only while you both touch. If one lets go, the newest rows unravel.

A speculative design prototype: what if a technology refused to work for one person alone? Nothing you do here leaves this device.

On a laptop: hold the A and L keys together.
